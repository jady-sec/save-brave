# Surface brief: Save Brave app (Operate)

## Direction contract

Replaces the earlier "Polymer banknote" direction (2026-10-29). The user pinned this reference:
https://uxpilot.ai/mobile-design-templates/budget-planner-ui. The look below follows it.

THESIS: Your month on one figure. Every screen answers "where do I stand" with the headline number first.

OWN-WORLD: Bold-outline pop (user reference, Nov 2026). Orange (#f47b20) header with rounded bottom corners carrying the title and the headline figure in black type. White page; cards, buttons, inputs and the floating pill nav all have a 2px #111 outline and a hard 3px 4px offset shadow (pressing moves the element into its shadow). Bright gradient stat tiles and coloured category squares are decoration; status keeps amber/red/green and purple for shared. Lexend type, tabular figures wherever money appears. Black text on every colour. Mark: a white savings jar (hard shadow, purple lid) holding a teal, a purple and a gold coin, on orange; on launch the coins drop in.

COLOUR: Orange header and buttons, bright decorative tiles and category squares. Status colour stays separate: amber at 80% of a limit, red when over, green when a target is met, a quiet purple tag for shared items. Status always also has text.

STORY: Open → the month's saved figure in the header, then the budget cards in a two-column grid, alerts above them. "+" always one thumb away.

SIGNATURE: The header-to-sheet seam: dark band, white sheet rising over it with the grab handle. The Add sheet repeats it.

RISK: The status colours drift into decoration; low-contrast grey text on white (the template's #8A8A8E fails 4.5:1, so body grey is darker).
